A little about me

Hi, I'm Noma.

Product designer.
Curious human.
Professional overthinker of things that probably didn't need overthinking.

I spend most of my working hours figuring out why something isn't working, how it could work better, and occasionally wondering why someone decided a six-step checkout was a good idea.

I design digital products, but I'm interested in much more than interfaces. I'm interested in people, businesses, ideas, places and the strange little details that make experiences memorable.

Design is
what I do. But it isn't
all I am.

I'm naturally curious about how things work — and why they don't. That curiosity shows up in my work. I like understanding people, questioning assumptions, untangling messy ideas and turning "we need a thing" into figuring out what the thing actually needs to be.

That's probably why I enjoy product design. There's always something underneath the interface worth investigating.

Outside the screen
Travel

I love to travel.

I like seeing new places, trying new things and being reminded that there is rarely only one way to experience something. Travel has a funny way of making you notice how differently people move through the world — something I find useful as a designer too.

Reading

I read. A lot.

Fiction, essays, psychology, business, creativity, biographies — if there's an interesting idea inside it, I'll probably want to read it. I like reading because it lets me spend time inside someone else's way of thinking.
